# Proposed native-task process successor

Status: proposal only. This text has no effect until the owner approves this exact
version. It applies only to the new orchestrated current-era and Cacti closeout.
It does not amend fixed40, its generator, or its admission and capture contracts.

Scope: synthetic mainnet-configuration and historical-era integration, including
actual mainnet numeric economics, delay and reward schedule, historical activation
fixtures, existing native/precompile/frame adapters, committed historical APIs,
synthetic concrete-owner initialization and bounded existing recovery callbacks.
The new implementation prompt and owner-approved scope addendum define the finite
matrix and reference boundaries. The owner-approved continuation permits a
recorded same-baseline allowance amendment and recovery after a failed bounded
command. Resource limits, data protection, validation and independent review
remain unchanged except for the exact closeout supplement below.

Transition closeout: finish source-bound current-era and Cacti acceptance and the
required package/storage/ON gates, then stop with a durable orchestration handoff.
The larger historical gate remains open. For this closeout only, the owner may
approve the cumulative retained/live task cap of 1,342,177,280 bytes (1.25 GiB),
a prospective 268,435,456-byte supplement. All old evidence, charges and any
historical failures remain preserved. No elapsed-time or output limit increases.

Successor-run authority: the owner-approved orchestrated closeout uses a NEW
planner-session baseline and NEW task envelope: 7,200 aggregate captured child
seconds, 67,108,864 combined uncompressed output bytes per command and
1,073,741,824 bytes of new retained evidence plus live scratch. Its fixed account
floor is 20% remaining. Derive its one baseline allowance from the same fresh
planner observation used for initialization. Helpers do not initialize separate
grants. This is explicit new-task authority, not transfer or refund of the old
implementation bank. The old session, budget, caps, counters, failures and
archived source/evidence remain immutable; do not resume it. New working-source
edits are confined to successor-owned paths; archived baseline bytes stay fixed. The earlier 256 MiB supplement applies only
to its closed run. Existing read-only inputs are excluded from NEW growth once;
all new copies, source/evidence/witness output and live scratch are counted.
The existing guarded success-only scratch rule applies prospectively to NEW
registered successor roots. Never register old outputs for disposal. All other
parity, capture, review, admission and validation rules remain binding. Stop the
new run at accepted current-era/Cacti closure, its account floor, resource bound
or required authority/input/unsafe-state blocker. The full historical gate stays
open; no Phala or other historical expansion is included.

1. Use one serial implementer. Start a helper only for a named independent
   deliverable. Luna startup and a reserved Luna slot are not required for this
   task. Keep independent review: Astra medium settles the new oracle/authority
   boundary; a separate Sol medium reviewer checks frozen implementation and
   evidence. A reviewer must not approve its own work. Check capacity and confirm
   requested model startup; record unknown runtime identity as unknown.
2. The owner supplies either a new allowance in percentage points or an explicit
   instruction to run without an allowance limit. There is no default grant.
   A bounded run initializes one fresh session baseline, then uses that same file.
   Keep identity, freshness, reset, rollback and stop checks in codex_quota.py.
   An explicit owner amendment may change only consume_percent and
   floor_remaining_percent for the same saved session/window. First preserve
   the original budget bytes and record both hashes and the exact authority;
   retain its baseline usage, timestamp, session and reset fields. Validate fresh
   matching telemetry before and after the amendment. Do not initialize or rebase.
   Never transfer the old implementer's unused allowance. Planning has no grant.
3. Use scripts/capture_command.py for evidence commands. Use new output paths,
   complete streams, status and child exit, source/input hashes, and nonzero
   matched test counts. Demonstrate real stderr and exit 7 once. A timeout,
   truncated stream, runner error or missing result is a failed check.
4. For this task, replace manual cumulative engineering read/unit/retention banks
   and actor-time transfers with this finite task envelope: at most 7,200 seconds
   of aggregate captured child-command elapsed time, at most 64 MiB combined
   stdout/stderr per command, and at most 1 GiB of newly retained task evidence,
   or the exact owner-approved transition-closeout cumulative cap above.
   Reference commands have a 900-second outer limit; focused tests/checks have
   600 seconds; complete affected-package gates and the required fast gate have
   1,800 seconds each. The aggregate limit also applies. Count failed commands.
   The implementation lead reads status.json after each command and stops before
   launching a command whose full timeout exceeds the remaining envelope. Sum
   recorded elapsed_seconds; an interrupted/unknown status blocks another start
   until reconciled, and its full timeout stays charged until then. Use a short
   task checkpoint, not per-actor reservations or a new accounting library.
   A per-command timeout, output overflow or runner failure remains failed
   immutable evidence. Once child termination and status are reconciled, the
   implementer may repair or partition that command and continue in the SAME
   remaining aggregate time, retention and owner allowance. Every retry is
   bounded and charged; no required cell is dropped and no oversized command
   is permitted. An unknown child/status still blocks dependent launches.
   Aggregate exhaustion, allowance floor and unresolved unsafe state still stop
   the task. This permission does not raise any command or task resource limit.
   Planning/review prose and idle time do not consume command seconds. Owner
   allowance observations still include account-wide use. Required validation
   remains required if a bound prevents completion: checkpoint with an open gate.
5. The 1 GiB bound (or approved closeout cap) covers evidence, fixture output
   and task scripts. It excludes
   existing source/input files, shared build caches and existing recovery copies.
   Do not delete old evidence to make space. Do not dump database bodies into
   routine handoffs. Disposable oracle source trees must be under the task's
   persistent artifact directory; existing input paths remain read-only.
   For the transition closeout, permit prospective success-only disposal of
   newly registered, exclusively task-owned test database scratch directories.
   Register each new root before creation; never register pre-authorization
   files, source, fixtures, capture streams or reference inputs as scratch.
   Preserve all existing outputs and every unexpected failure/incomplete run.
   An expected-error negative may dispose its new private copy only after all
   error/isolation assertions pass and its exact mutation/result is retained.
   Close all database handles and finish every reopen/recovery/dependent check
   before disposal. Emit a durable source-bound case result, input/configuration
   pins, root/receipt/native/history/recovery comparison results and retention
   disposition before cleanup. Keep the designated final-source qualification
   pairs for independent inspection; do not dispose them as routine scratch.
   Validate root ownership/containment and reject symlinks or unrelated paths;
   successful cases may clean only their registered roots. No cleanup on panic,
   unexpected error, unknown completion or missing witness. A later package
   failure does not erase the recorded success of an earlier cleaned case.
   Count all live scratch against the cap and peak-space admission. Disposal
   releases future working capacity, never refunds elapsed time, quota, old
   evidence or the recorded peak. Preserve capture streams/statuses/manifests
   and all retained-case identities. Do not change assertions or replace fresh
   execution/recovery with an API-only reused pair to obtain cleanup success.

   Check fresh telemetry and exact source/input hashes immediately before a gate.

   fails to close its named acceptance gate, classify the cause as implementation,
